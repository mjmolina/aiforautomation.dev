About me

From Molecular Biology to Machine Learning

I am a Senior Data Scientist with a PhD Biotech and eight years applying machine learning in industry: classical NLP, deep learning for vision and time series, and more recently LLM agents and retrieval systems in production.

My doctoral work integrated transcriptomic, proteomic, genomic and physiological data to study plant proximity responses. The move into data science was less of a jump than it sounds: the constant has been working with messy scientific data and asking whether a result actually holds.

Since 2018 I have led projects end to end: scoping, hiring, modelling and deployment, across different industries.
